# Important notices — RMTI Sandbox (Disaster Science)

This file states plainly what the software is for and what it is not for. It is a statement of intended use, **not legal advice**.

## 1. Intended use

This software is provided for teaching, methodological demonstration and academic research on the Risk Mechanism Theory Index (RMTI).

## 2. Not a decision tool for emergencies, safety or engineering

- It is not a validated emergency-management, civil-defense, evacuation-planning, life-safety or structural-engineering tool.
- It is not a geospatial hazard model and does not replace hazard mapping.
- Its scores are relative, rubric-based indices. They are **not** forecasts, engineering assessments, insurance or actuarial figures, or investment advice.

## 3. Professional assessments come first

Planning, mitigation, resource allocation and response decisions should rest on professionally validated hazard and risk assessments, applicable government specifications and regulatory criteria. Nothing shown by this tool should be used in place of them.

## 4. Worked examples are illustrations

The city examples (Bamako, Lagos, Lilongwe, Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Lusaka) show how the method works. Their inputs are teaching illustrations. They are not official assessments of those cities and should not be quoted as such.

## 5. Independence

This is an independent academic tool. It is not an official product of the World Bank Group or of any other institution, and it carries no institutional endorsement.
